Shift participation agreement

Version 1, November 2026

Each organisation accepts this once, before its members save maps on the Shift server. It makes the organisation the controller of what it records and The MEL Barn the processor that holds it, which UK GDPR requires in writing (Article 28). It does not apply to Shift used without an account, where maps never leave the browser.

Who is who

  • The organisation is the one named when it is created in Shift, acting through its admins. It decides what goes into its maps and is the controller for those records.
  • The MEL Barn means Shift, a MEL Barn tool, run by Koushik Raghavan. It holds and handles the organisation's records only on the organisation's behalf, as its processor.
  • MEL Barn accounts (sign-in details and access requests) are The MEL Barn's own records, covered by the Shift privacy notice, not by this agreement.

What Shift handles for the organisation

Shift handles these records so the organisation can map the stakeholders it is trying to move and see whether they moved. They are kept until the organisation deletes them or asks The MEL Barn to.

What The MEL Barn does

  1. Acts only on the organisation's instructions. This agreement and what its members do in Shift are those instructions. If one seems to break data protection law, The MEL Barn says so.
  2. Keeps records confidential. Only Koushik Raghavan, and anyone later helping run the service under a duty of confidence, can read them, and only to run, secure or fix the service or answer a request.
  3. Keeps them secure. Records are encrypted in transit and on the provider's disks. Each organisation sees only its own records, enforced in the database. Recorded history cannot be edited.
  4. Uses only these providers: Supabase (database and sign-in, London), GitHub (serves the pages) and Google Workspace (sign-in emails). It gives 30 days' notice before adding or changing one, and the organisation may object or leave.
  5. Helps with people's rights. It passes on any request about the organisation's records within 5 working days and helps answer it. The organisation can download its records at any time.
  6. Reports breaches. It tells the organisation without undue delay, and within 48 hours of finding out, about any breach affecting its records, with what is known.
  7. Helps with other duties. It gives the information the organisation needs for its own records or a data protection impact assessment.
  8. Deletes at the end. When the organisation leaves, it can download its maps first. The MEL Barn deletes the organisation's records within 30 days of being asked. No backups are kept at present; if that changes, admins hear first and backup copies will expire on a stated schedule.
  9. Shows how this is kept. It answers the organisation's reasonable questions about how its records are handled.

What the organisation does

  1. Works for animals. None of its members works for animal farming, its suppliers or its trade bodies, the same rule as for MEL Barn access.
  2. Has its own lawful basis for what it records about people, and tells them where the law expects it to.
  3. Maps organisations and public roles, not named people. A map saved on the server names "the farming minister" or "the regional officer", not the person. Reasoning and notes hold no one's health, religion, political opinions or similar details.
  4. Keeps its own copy. No backups are kept at present, so the organisation downloads its maps from the Data tab as often as it needs to.
  5. Keeps its member list right. Admins remove people who leave, give the admin role only to people they trust, and treat invite links like passwords.
  6. Reports problems. It tells The MEL Barn about any breach or misuse it notices.

The service

  • Free and in development. Shift costs nothing and is still being built, so features change. Admins hear by email before any change to how records are used.
  • Changes to this agreement are emailed to admins 30 days before they apply. An organisation that disagrees can leave.
  • Ending. Either side can end this at any time by email. The MEL Barn may end it at once if the organisation breaks point 1 of the section above.
  • Law. The law of England and Wales.
