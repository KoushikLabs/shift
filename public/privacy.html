Shift privacy notice

This version: November 2026

Who we are

Shift, a MEL Barn tool, is run by Koushik Raghavan, who is the controller for your MEL Barn account and for the service's own records. Write to privacy@themelbarn.org with any privacy question or request.

Shift helps organisations working for animals map the stakeholders they are trying to move, and keep a record of whether anyone moved. You can use it in two ways, and the app always shows which one you are in.

  • Without an account. Your maps stay in your own browser. Nothing you type is sent to us, and we cannot see it. Most of this notice does not apply to you; see "Browser storage and cookies" below.
  • Signed in. You sign in with a MEL Barn account, which also opens other MEL Barn tools such as Bellwether. Your organisation's maps are stored on our server so that your colleagues can see them. The rest of this notice is about this way of using Shift.

Your organisation decides what it records in its Shift maps and is the controller for those records. We act on its behalf, under the participation agreement an admin accepts for the organisation.

Why we use it

We use your information to run the service your organisation chose to use, to decide who may join, and to keep it secure. Under UK GDPR our lawful basis is legitimate interests for each purpose below.

PurposeLawful basis
Run your account and your organisation's maps, including sign-in and password emailsLegitimate interests: providing the service your organisation asked for
Decide on access requests, and keep the tools for people working for animalsLegitimate interests: keeping a movement tool out of the hands of the industries it scrutinises
Keep the service secureLegitimate interests: security and accountability
Records your organisation keeps about other peopleYour organisation's own lawful basis; we act on its instructions

A score for where someone stands on a political question, written down beside their name, is sensitive personal data, and that person has a right to see it. For this reason signed-in maps take organisations and public roles, not named people, and we ask you not to record anyone's health, religion, political opinions or similar details in your reasoning or notes. If you need to map a named person, use Shift without an account, where the map never leaves your browser and your organisation alone is responsible for it.

Who can see it

Your organisation's maps are seen by its own members and by the service operator, and by no other organisation.

  • Your organisation's members see and can edit every map in the organisation, and see each other's names and email addresses. Admins also manage members and invite links.
  • Other organisations never see your maps. The database refuses to return one organisation's records to another.
  • Anyone holding an unused invite link can join your organisation and see its maps, so treat a link like a password. Links expire after 14 days and an admin can revoke them.
  • The service operator can read stored records through the hosting provider's tools. Records are encrypted on the provider's disks, but there is no extra layer of encryption that would stop the operator reading them. We read them only to run, secure or fix the service, or to answer a request.
  • Our providers handle information only to provide their service to us (next section).
  • Anyone else only where the law requires it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, export or delete your information, or object to how we use it, and we reply within one month. These are your rights under UK GDPR.

  • About your organisation's maps: ask your organisation's admin first, since your organisation decides what it records.
  • About your account, an access request or anything else: write to privacy@themelbarn.org.
  • We may need to confirm who you are, and we check requests so they do not expose other people's information.
  • If you are unhappy with our answer, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Browser storage and cookies

Shift sets no cookies and loads no analytics, advertising or font scripts. The page talks only to the address it was served from and, when you are signed in, to Supabase.

  • Without an account, your maps are kept in this browser's own database on your device. Clearing this site's data in your browser deletes them, so download a copy from the Data tab if you want to keep them.
  • Signed in, your sign-in session and the organisation you last opened are kept in your browser's local storage until you sign out.
  • In both cases your browser keeps your light or dark setting and a copy of the app itself, so that Shift opens when you are offline.
